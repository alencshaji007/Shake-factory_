    About the factory

    We don't make milkshakes. We engineer cravings.

    Every shake is built like a commercial is shot: one hero ingredient at a time. Real fruit is hand-cut to order, nuts are roasted in-house, chocolate is melted, not pumped. Then fresh milk, a lot of noise, and a glass that won't stay dry for long.
